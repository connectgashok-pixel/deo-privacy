# DEO-privacy
Deo – Privacy Policy
Deo is a personal expense tracker for family use.

Your data stays in your own Google Drive. The app stores your transactions, settings and attachments only in spreadsheets and folders it creates in your Google Drive. The developer has no server and cannot see your data.

Google access. The app asks only for access to files it creates in your Drive (the drive.file permission). It cannot see your other Drive files.

AI features (optional). If you turn on AI features, the text you paste, your voice recordings, or your search question are sent to Google Gemini using your own API key, to extract or search transactions. Nothing is sent when AI is off.

On your phone. Your Gemini key and a cached copy of your data are stored encrypted on your phone and removed when you sign out.

No tracking. The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking.
